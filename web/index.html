THE EXPLORER’S GUIDE

How to make it out.

Collect all 5 crystals, then step into the gate in the top-right corner. Your torch lasts 50 successful moves.

  • MoveArrow keys, W A S D, or the touch pad. No Enter key needed.
  • Stay aliveSpikes take 2 hearts each time you enter. The potion restores up to 3, capped at 5, and is consumed on pickup.
  • Take a breathWalls, pauses, and thinking cost nothing. Press Esc or P to pause, R to restart, H for this guide.
  • Make it countEscape with 10 points per unused move and 20 per remaining heart. The last torch turn still counts!

UNDER THE SURFACE

One engine,
two frontends.

Every rule lives in C++17, compiled to WebAssembly: movement, collisions, damage, the torch, and your score. The same engine still runs the original terminal game.

The cave artwork is drawn in Canvas 2D. JavaScript handles animation, input, synthesized audio, and your locally saved best score. There is no game framework, no art to download, and no backend.
